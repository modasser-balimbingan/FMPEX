USER-CENTERED ACCESS

The right tools for every type of user.

Farmers are the primary users, while buyers/vendors, administrators, and guests receive role-appropriate access.

PRIMARY USER

Farmer

Register and manage an account, create and manage produce listings, view market prices and weather, configure alerts, receive notifications, and mark produce as sold or inactive.

BROWSE & SEARCH

Buyer / Vendor

Browse available produce, search for products, filter by category or location, and view listing details.

MONITOR & MANAGE

Administrator

Manage accounts, roles, categories, listings, market-price records, alerts, notifications, activity logs, and integrations.

PUBLIC ACCESS

Guest

Browse public listings, search available produce, and view basic produce information without modifying protected data.

Browse as Guest
